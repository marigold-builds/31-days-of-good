# UN observance dates for October 2026 (reference)

**Nothing on this page is scheduled, assigned or committed.** `data/calendar.json` — the file the site actually builds from — carries only a day number and a date for each of October 2026's 31 nights. A night's SDG and project are chosen during that night's own Discover phase, from the intake inbox and the ideas that surface that night (see [../docs/02-daily-process.md](../docs/02-daily-process.md) and [../docs/11-intake-and-triage.md](../docs/11-intake-and-triage.md)). See Decided 21 in [../docs/07-decisions.md](../docs/07-decisions.md).

What follows is a list of the UN observance days falling in October 2026, verified against un.org (see [../docs/09-research-notes.md](../docs/09-research-notes.md)). These are objective facts — World Food Day is 16 October whether or not this programme exists — and are offered as context a night's research may find useful when casting about for a problem space. They carry no implication that a particular night will address a particular SDG or research a particular idea. Candidate projects are organised by SDG in [seed-ideas.md](seed-ideas.md), not by date.

## Notes on the observance dates (facts, kept for reference)

- World Statistics Day is only observed every five years and does not fall in 2026.
- World Habitat Day is the first Monday of October, so 5 October in 2026; it collides with World Teachers' Day. A night's research is free to use it on either date, or not at all.
- Global Handwashing Day is WHO/UNICEF-backed rather than a UN General Assembly day; still a possible hook for research that reaches for it.

## History of this file

Until 7 September 2026 this page was a proposed day-by-day schedule with an SDG, archetype and seed project assigned to every night. `data/calendar.json` carried these assignments as live data that the site rendered as though 31 projects were already decided. Dom ruled that this contradicted the programme's own demand-first design (Decided 17) and struck the assignment (Decided 21). The SDG, archetype and seed columns were then removed entirely (8 September 2026) to eliminate any appearance that a night's work is pre-steered. This page is now kept only as a reference to the UN observance calendar for October 2026.
