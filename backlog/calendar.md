## Notes

- World Statistics Day (20 Oct) is only observed every five years and does not fall in 2026.
- World Habitat Day is the first Monday of October, so 5 October in 2026; it collides with World Teachers' Day and is carried to the 6th.
- Global Handwashing Day is WHO/UNICEF-backed rather than a UN General Assembly day; still a strong hook.
- The mid-month maintenance sprint sits on Sunday 18 October, after the dense 13–17 observance cluster, and pairs with a low-risk dataset archetype.
